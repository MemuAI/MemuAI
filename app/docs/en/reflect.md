# Reflect, time and focus

**Settings → Reflect** is a calm look back: when and on what you spend time with MemuAI, and how you work with it. It isn't a grade, it's a mirror — what to change is up to you.

## Opening it

Open **Settings → Reflect**. A report is made only at that moment and is saved, so opening it again is instant. Pick the period at the top — "This month so far", "Last month" (the default), "Past 3 months", "Past 6 months" or "Past year". The ↻ button next to it writes the report again. If there have been new conversations since, an "Update the recap" link appears above the report.

## What the report shows

- **Opening** — a headline and a paragraph about your actual work and themes in the period.
- **Your time with AI** — your most active weekday, peak hour, number of conversations, a daily activity chart and an estimate of the time spent with AI. Time is estimated from message times: a pause over 20 minutes starts a new session. MemuAI counts these numbers itself, without a model.
- **What you spent time on** — topics with a short description and a share in percent.
- **Expanding your skills** — four cards of the 4D AI Fluency framework: *Delegation* (what to hand off), *Description* (the context you give), *Discernment* (how you weigh answers) and *Diligence* (how you own the result). Each pairs an observation from your chats with a concrete next step — for example a [Work project](doc:work), a system prompt or a skill instead of re-explaining context every time.
- **Something to reflect on** — a question that changes every week. "Talk it through" opens a new chat with it.
- 👍/👎 at the bottom — tell whether the recap looks right. The vote stays on your computer.

The summary, topics and observations are written by the model in **Model for the report** (the chat model by default). Topics and shares are AI estimates and can be imprecise.

## What's included and what isn't

- Saved MemuAI chats (regular, Work and MemuCode) and, unless turned off, [Quick Ask](doc:quick-ask) and Telegram chats.
- **Temporary chats are never included.**
- Attachments, files and images aren't read — only the text of conversations.
- The model only gets titles and short excerpts of your messages, never whole chats.
- Personal and difficult topics are never sent as excerpts, never lead the report and never get percentages or counts. Where appropriate, support resources appear next to the report.
- The report stays in the MemuAI data folder and isn't used for anything else. **Turn on Reflect** switches the page off and deletes saved reports.

## Time and focus

**Settings → Time & focus** (or the "Set quiet hours and breaks" link in the report). These are boundaries you choose, not limits — you can always dismiss them. They work independently of Reflect.

- **Quiet hours** — days of the week and a time window (say 22:00–07:00). If you send a message or open Quick Ask during it, MemuAI gently reminds you: "Continue" hides the reminder until the window ends, "Come back later" keeps the message in the box. A window across midnight belongs to the day it starts on.
- **Break reminder** — once you have spent the chosen time with MemuAI in a day (the main window, Quick Ask and Telegram add up), a quiet card appears: "Take a break" (the next reminder comes after the same time again), "In 10 minutes" or "Dismiss" (for the rest of the day). Pauses over 10 minutes don't count; the count resets at midnight.
- **In Telegram** quiet hours and breaks apply too: the bot holds the first message of a quiet window and asks "Continue?", and the Mini App asks the same before sending.
